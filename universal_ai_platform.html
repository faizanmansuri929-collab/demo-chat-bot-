Advanced settings (technical)
  • Vector index + semantic retrieval — the AI template builds a searchable index of your pages and finds relevant answers by meaning.
  • Deterministic FAQ mode — the Instant Answers template pre-generates answers once, so runtime has zero per-message LLM calls.
  • Sitemap discovery — sitemap.xml is preferred when available; otherwise pages are followed from the homepage within the chosen scope.
